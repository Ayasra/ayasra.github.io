Pursuing a PhD in Wireless Communication, I am an R&D Engineer at TurkTelekom (Oredata). My work involves shaping the future of mobile technology by making direct contributions to 3GPP standards for 6G and conducting in-depth research. My primary areas of expertise are intelligent surfaces and transceiver design for future mobile generations, with a strong recent focus on network energy saving strategies. Originally from Jordan, I possess comprehensive experience in communication systems theory, complemented by extensive hands-on programming experience.
